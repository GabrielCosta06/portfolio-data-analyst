Definitions and decisions

Revenue = quantity × unit price. Gross profit = revenue minus direct cost. Margin = total gross profit ÷ total revenue. The pipeline stores monetary values as integer cents.

Across the full dataset, Equipment leads in revenue and Services contributes the most gross profit. Comparing both metrics helps decide which category to prioritize. The synthetic data illustrates the method.

Each order has one line. The model excludes taxes, returns, shipping and discounts. Filters cover complete months. The analysis is descriptive.
