    Data and comparison rules

    Match issuer, consolidated scope, reporting dates, currency and account code. Convert the published unit scale to BRL cents before comparing. The tracker distinguishes amounts, descriptions, new lines and removed lines. Earnings per share are outside the currency comparison.

    Account deltas include detail and subtotal lines. Capture a new archive to preserve its selected records under the archive fingerprint and rebuild the comparisons.
